# Practice · Correct a summary and appraise P3

Complete this page before opening the [answers and scoring guide](answer-key.en.md). Use public papers only. Patient information, a paid model and an AI account are not required. Paper and pencil or a spreadsheet are enough for every task.

**The question you are answering:**

> In case-based assessment tasks, how does clinicians' diagnostic reasoning or differential diagnosis performance with large language model assistance compare with their performance using conventional resources?

P1, P2 and P3 are [fixed teaching selections](sources.en.md), not a complete search or systematic review. First decide whether each study addresses this question, then decide what conclusion you can write.

## Exercise 1 · Correct six errors · 12 points

**The following text was deliberately written with errors for this course. It is not a record of a real AI run or a statement of the papers' conclusions. Each label identifies one error to correct. Do not quote it as evidence or use it for clinical decisions.**

> [E1] P1 had a sample size of 50 cases.
>
> [E2] P3 studied consultations with real patients in routine outpatient care.
>
> [E3] P3 compared diagnostic performance between physicians using AI assistance and physicians without AI.
>
> [E4] The between-group P value of .60 in P1 proves that AI assistance and conventional resources are exactly equivalent.
>
> [E5] The adjusted between-group difference in P1 was a relative improvement of 2%.
>
> [E6] These studies have established that clinicians using AI in real clinical care reduce diagnostic errors and improve patient outcomes.

For each of **E1–E6**, explain what is wrong, write a correction and give its source. If you cannot locate supporting evidence, write **To be checked**. Do not invent numbers, quotations or page numbers. Give the original section, table or course-card field you actually read.

Copy this format once per label:

```text
Error ID: E__
Problem and correction:
Evidence: Paper P__; original section/table or course-card field:
Verification status: Relevant original sections checked / Abstract only /
Course card only; original text not yet checked
```

Each item is worth 2 points; see the answer page for the criteria. If AI helps organize your response, you still need to check its correction and source. Without an AI account, read the materials and complete the same fields yourself.

## Exercise 2 · Check P2's relevance · Guided, no additional points

Read [P2's source card](sources.en.md#p2) and its indicated original sections. Add one row to the same [evidence table](../../templates/evidence-table.en.csv), then answer:

1. What material did the physicians receive? What do the physician count and case count each represent?
2. Which condition involved physicians using AI, and what was the actual comparator? Keep model-alone performance separate from the collaborative comparison.
3. What aspect of performance on case tasks was measured? Does it directly support improved patient outcomes?

Record the extent of your actual source checking. With P1 and P2 complete, move on to P3 independently. Leave any unread fields **To be checked**.

## Independent assignment · What does P3 tell us? · 8 points

Open [P3's source card](sources.en.md#p3) and the public full text. Complete both outputs below independently, before looking at the answers. Preserve the study's actual design rather than rewriting it as a collaboration trial it did not conduct.

### Output 1 · One P3 evidence row

Use the [evidence table CSV](../../templates/evidence-table.en.csv), retaining these 12 column names:

```text
Paper ID,Title,Year,DOI,Study setting,Participants and sample units,Intervention and comparator,Outcome measures,Main results,Source location,Limitations,Verification status
```

Your row should answer:

- Who or what was compared?
- Was the setting actual care, written cases or simulated dialogue?
- Does each number count physicians, cases, scenarios or another unit?
- What did the main outcome actually measure?

Provide traceable locations for at least **setting/sample units**, **comparison conditions**, and **main results**. Do not add unreported data merely to fill the table.

### Output 2 · A conclusion of 150–200 English words

**Count English words, not Chinese characters.** The English and Chinese versions have separate length instructions; they do not assume that a Chinese-character count equals an English-word count. Use your word processor or count whitespace-separated words. For this exercise, a hyphenated term counts as one word. The worked answer uses the same whitespace-based count.

Explain:

- What P3's design and results can support.
- Whether it directly answers the lesson question or provides indirect/background evidence, and why.
- Which claims about clinicians using AI and real clinical outcomes still need other studies.

You can start from this outline, but complete it and check the evidence yourself:

If the full text will not open, you may use the course card. Name the card fields under **Source location** and retain **Course card only; original text not yet checked** as the status. Do not mark this as a full-text check.
