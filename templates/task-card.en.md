# Task card · Define the work before asking AI

Copy this card into your own document. Brackets mark fields for you to complete; they are not a finished study design.

- **Work to complete:** [For example: a departmental literature presentation]
- **Question in one sentence:** [Population + setting + comparison + outcome to observe]
- **Study or assessment population:** [Clinicians, patients, cases or tools; identify each separately]
- **Task setting:** [Written cases, simulated dialogue, real clinical care or another setting]
- **Intervention/tool:** [Who uses what; whether a person participates]
- **Comparator:** [Who or which condition it is compared with]
- **Outcomes of interest:** [Specific measures and units]
- **Required output:** [Evidence table, one-page explanation or another output]
- **Material you can provide:** [Public paper links, course material or institutionally permitted content]
- **Scope:** [What the conclusion should address and which unmeasured outcomes are outside it]
- **Human verification method:** [Original source locations, calculation checks, colleague review, etc.]
- **Still uncertain:** [Questions to ask or information to verify]
