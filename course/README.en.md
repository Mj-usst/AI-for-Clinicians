# Choose a clinical work task

**AI for Clinicians / 医生 AI 实战课** is designed for clinicians with no coding experience. Each lesson follows **work task → demonstration → check the result → independent assignment**, with a planned length of 45–60 minutes.

## Lesson 1 · Use AI for the first time

**Status: planned.** Learn to describe a task, provide the necessary material, set output requirements, ask follow-up questions and check the result. Planned output: a reusable task template.

## Lesson 2 · Turn a clinical question into research evidence

**Status: sample lesson available.** Use three public research papers to practise keeping a search log, organizing evidence, setting conclusion boundaries and correcting an AI-style summary. Outputs: an evidence table, corrections with reasons and sources, and a short conclusion.

[Start learning](../lessons/02-evidence/README.en.md) · [Get ready](../START_HERE.en.md) · [Instructor guide](../lessons/02-evidence/instructor.en.md)

## Lesson 3 · Work with research tables and charts

**Status: planned.** Use synthetic data to practise creating a data dictionary, checking missing values, summarizing data and making charts. Planned outputs: a cleaned table and chart explanations that can be checked against their source.

## Lesson 4 · Turn a research idea into a discussable plan

**Status: planned.** Define the study population, outcomes, variables and data sources, and learn how to communicate with statistical or algorithm collaborators. Planned outputs: a one-page research plan and a list of collaboration needs.

## Lesson 5 · Prepare manuscripts, presentations and teaching material

**Status: planned.** Create an abstract, departmental presentation, handout or public information material from established facts. Planned output: a sourced draft ready for further revision.

## Lesson 6 · Take part in a medical AI project

**Status: planned.** Use medical imaging examples to understand annotation, data splitting, model evaluation and clinical validation. Planned output: a checklist for project collaboration and paper appraisal.

Later lessons will be adjusted in response to sample-lesson feedback. Release dates for unfinished content are not promised.
