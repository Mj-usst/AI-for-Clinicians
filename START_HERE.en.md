# Start here: complete one lesson

**AI for Clinicians / 医生 AI 实战课** is for clinicians with no coding experience. Start with a practical lesson in checking research evidence. You can learn it on its own, without reading the whole repository or studying machine learning first.

## Gather what you need

- About **55 minutes**, a browser, and paper or a spreadsheet for notes.
- Optional: an AI chat tool you already use. Text input is enough; paid features, web search and file uploads are not required.
- Open the [lesson](lessons/02-evidence/README.en.md) and [source cards](lessons/02-evidence/sources.en.md).
- Download the [evidence table](templates/evidence-table.en.csv) and [search log](templates/search-log.en.csv). If your spreadsheet displays them incorrectly, use **Import from Text/CSV** and select **UTF-8**.

## Complete these five tasks

1. **Read the goal and key terms.** Begin with the [lesson](lessons/02-evidence/README.en.md); Lesson 1 is not a prerequisite.
2. **Build one row with P1.** Read its source card and the indicated original sections, then fill in the evidence table.
3. **Compare two more studies.** Check whether each examines clinicians using AI or compares AI alone with clinicians.
4. **Do the assignment.** Open the [practice page](lessons/02-evidence/practice.en.md), correct an intentionally inaccurate summary, and appraise P3 independently.
5. **Check and revise.** Use the [answers and scoring guide](lessons/02-evidence/answer-key.en.md) and [evidence checklist](templates/evidence-checklist.en.md), then save your revision.

You will finish with an evidence table, six corrections with reasons and sources, and a short conclusion explaining the evidence boundaries.

## Use another route if an account or full text is unavailable

- **No AI account:** use the course-written inaccurate summary as the material to check. You can still complete the table and corrections. This practises evidence checking, although it does not provide hands-on experience using an AI tool.
- **Full text will not open:** try the alternative public links on the [source page](lessons/02-evidence/sources.en.md). If access still fails, work from the course cards and record **Course card only; original text not yet checked** under Verification status.
- **Abstract only:** record **Abstract only** and mark unread fields **To be checked**. Your status must describe the reading you actually completed.

This lesson does not require patient identifiers, medical records or original imaging. When applying the method to real work, follow your institution's data management rules.
